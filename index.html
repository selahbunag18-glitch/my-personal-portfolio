About

Learning by building.

I'm a 4th-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Nueva Ecija University of Science and Technology, and an aspiring web developer. I enjoy creating modern, functional, and user-friendly websites and web applications — and I'm still very much in the process of getting good at it.

Most of what I know comes from doing: personal projects, coursework, webinars, and a lot of trial and error in the browser console. I work mainly with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend, and PHP with Laravel and MySQL on the backend, using Git, GitHub, XAMPP, and VS Code as my day-to-day tools.

I've also taken on real client work — building a business website for JF Villiamor focused on Facebook page growth and lead generation.

Right now my focus is simple: keep building real things, keep learning from them, and keep growing toward being a capable, dependable web developer.
